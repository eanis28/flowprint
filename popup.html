Before you begin

See patterns without recording your content.

Flowprint only observes after you press Start observing. It records when you move between websites and use actions such as click, copy, or paste.

What Flowprint savesThe time, type of action, and website name—such as google.com. It does not save the full page address.
What Flowprint never savesWords you type or copy, page contents, form answers, passwords, screenshots, or private documents.

Everything stays in this browser. You can exclude websites, stop observing at any time, and delete your data.

Observation offNothing is being recorded.
